## 🎬 Origin story

- **RMB founded: 2006** (per Spike) — by **Doug Kamo + Simon Green**.
- The **Queenstown Christmas Show** as a wider production began in **2007** (per ODT 2010 + ODT 2025), the year after RMB. RMB likely became the show's signature cameo from year one of the formal Show.
- **2026 is the 20th anniversary** of the RMB itself.

## 🟢 Bio drafts (press-sourced; needs verification by the people themselves)

### Doug Kamo · founder

> *25-year entertainment professional, MC, producer-director of more than 60 national/international musical theatre productions, founder of DKCM (Doug Kamo Creative Management). Started the Remarkable Men's Ballet with Simon Green.*

Doug Kamo is one of the busiest producers, directors and entertainment practitioners in the country. As director and producer he's overseen 60+ musical theatre productions, including *Wicked*, *Mamma Mia*, *Grease: The Arena Spectacular*, *Mary Poppins*, *Priscilla Queen of the Desert*, *The Rocky Horror Show*, *Les Misérables*, *The Producers*, and *The Addams Family*. He toured New Zealand and Asia in the international casts of *Chicago* and *Joseph and the Amazing Technicolor Dreamcoat*.

On the broadcast side he's hosted *Lotto* for two-and-a-half years, a string of travel and game shows, and breakfast for More FM Queenstown — and currently for *Doug & P* on The Hits Dunedin, the most-awarded regional breakfast show in New Zealand. He's done guest spots on *Xena Warrior Princess*, *Shortland Street* and *The Pretender*. As MC he handles around 40 conferences, awards and fundraisers a year for clients including Vodafone, Air NZ and Coca-Cola.

Out of all of which: he and Simon Green started the Remarkable Men's Ballet, and it's been going strong for nearly 20 years.

**Sources:**

- [Otago's Doug Kamo Joins The Hits Dunedin Breakfast Show](https://www.scoop.co.nz/stories/CU2012/S00139/otagos-doug-kamo-joins-the-hits-dunedin-breakfast-show.htm)

### Simon Green · founder

> *Concert director of the Shotover Jet Remarkable Christmas Spectacular. Festival director, Queenstown Winter Festival. The other half of the duo (with Doug Kamo) that kicked off the Remarkable Men's Ballet.*

In November 2010 Simon was named concert director for the fourth annual Shotover Jet Christmas Spectacular, joining Margaret O'Hanlon and Anna Stuart in what the ODT called a "creative dream team". The same year, the show featured 150+ local performers including the Queenstown Community Choir, Screaming Banshees, Sequin Sisters, the Remarkable Christmas Ensemble children's choir, 60+ dancers from Queenstown School of Dance, and — of course — the Remarkables Christmas Male Ballet Company.

He's also been Festival Director for the Queenstown Winter Festival, putting him at the centre of two of the town's biggest community events.

**Sources:**
- ["Dream team" organising Christmas spectacular](https://www.odt.co.nz/regions/queenstown-lakes/dream-team-organising-christmas-spectacular), ODT, 17 Nov 2010 (Tracey Roxburgh)
- Spike (founding-pair attribution)

### Craig "Ferg" Ferguson

> *Long-serving Queenstown radio broadcaster, four-term councillor, and the man whose dodgy knee keeps almost-but-not-quite ending the Remarkable Men's Ballet every year.*

Raised in Hamilton, Craig played rugby for the New Zealand Colts and the Junior All Blacks before "keeping Jock Hobbs out of age-group rugby" — until he got injured and Hobbs took the chance to become an All Blacks captain. After arriving in Queenstown for a ski holiday in '87, Craig stayed: liftie at Coronet Peak, wine waiter at Skyline, tour driver, then captain of Wakatipu Premiers rugby.

Station boss Chas Drader poached him from the tour bus into a job at Q92FM, and he's been on Queenstown breakfast radio ever since — currently with The Hits, marking 30+ years on local airwaves. He's chaired the Bruce Grant Youth Trust since its founding, MC'd more community fundraisers than he can count, and has served on the Queenstown Lakes District Council since 2013 (four terms and counting).

He's been on the Remarkable Men's Ballet roster since at least 2011, and as of 2024 was the troupe's "head mincer". He admits his dancing career is "a bit limited now — dodgy old left knee from rugby days," but he keeps showing up anyway.

**Sources:**

### Bert Haines

> *Co-owner of Future Hospitality Group — the people behind Margo's, Little Blackwood, Minus 5° ICE BAR and Rhino's Ski Shack. Hospitality icon. Apparently has put on a tutu in his time.*

Bert is one of Queenstown's most established hospitality figures. He and business partner James Ace took advantage of the pandemic's quiet stretch to open two new venues in 2020, and Future Hospitality Group has since grown to seven venues across the resort. They run Mexican-inspired Margo's, lakeside bar Little Blackwood, the family-friendly Minus 5° ICE BAR, and hip-hop joint Rhino's Ski Shack. Bert was a finalist for Hospitality Leader of the Year at the 2024 Hospitality New Zealand Awards For Excellence.

### James Beech

> *ODT journalist whose first-person column on the 2011 RMB rehearsal is, twelve years on, the most useful list of cast names we've got.*

In December 2011, James wrote the "Lissom lads" column for the Otago Daily Times as an embedded participant in the Remarkable Men's Ballet — describing the costume reveal ("duct tape will be needed at the back of mine while I'm flouncing around otherwise our show will instantly go from ballet to burlesque"), Anna Stuart's saintly patience as choreographer, and the manoeuvre where he and Ferg hoist Rowan McDonald into the air.

The full 2011 lineup he documented: Craig Ferguson, Graeme Jackson, Paul van der Kaag, Tony Moore, Simon Thew, Henry Youngman, Irik Anderson, Darren Craig, JD Marrable, Chris Dagg, Andrew Bisset, and James himself.

### Glyn Lewers

> *Sitting Queenstown Lakes District Mayor — elected 2022. The current chain-of-office gets to put on a tutu and that, frankly, is wonderful.*

Glyn Lewers won the Queenstown Lakes mayoralty in October 2022 (succeeding Jim Boult) after one term as a councillor, and has signalled he's running again in 2025. He came to politics from a background in community involvement, with stated priorities around community at the heart of council decisions.

That now makes **three confirmed RMB politicians**: Glyn (current mayor), the late Clive Geddes (former mayor, 2009 performer), and Peter Laurenson (husband of former mayor Vanessa van Uden, 2024 performer). The troupe seems to have a knack for collecting mayors.

### Shaun Drader

> *Queenstown police constable. Almost certainly a Drader from THE Drader family — Chas (his father / uncle?) founded Q92FM and was Craig Ferguson's broadcasting mentor.*

A 2009 CNN tech article credited Constable Sean Drader for pioneering the Queenstown police's use of Facebook for community engagement — at the time he worked as an intelligence analyst for QPD. The family had skied at Coronet Peak for over 10 years; his son's name is Solomon.

The Drader connection is too neat to be coincidence: Chas Drader (Q92FM founder, "the Godfather of Radio" per Lakes Weekly Bulletin) was Ferg's broadcasting mentor. So Shaun likely has long-standing ties to the same Queenstown community circle the RMB came out of.
