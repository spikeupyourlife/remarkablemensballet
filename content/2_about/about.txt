Headline: How this happened

----

Body:

It started, as most regrettable ideas do, in a pub.

Twenty years on, the Remarkable Men's Ballet has become one of the most-anticipated cameos in the Queenstown Christmas Show. Every December, a rotating cast of locals — tradies, professionals, retirees, the occasional ring-in — squeeze into tutus they almost certainly shouldn't be wearing, and perform a single dance routine for two and a half thousand of their closest friends.

We do it for the kids. We do it for the laugh. We do it because once a year, the whole town turns up and Queenstown — for one strange and wonderful evening — agrees that this is exactly what it needed.

This site is the new home for everything: the photos, the videos, the cast lists, the press, twenty years of glorious bad decisions.

Welcome.
