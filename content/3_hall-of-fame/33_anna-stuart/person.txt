Bio: 

The choreographer with the patience of a saint, and the woman who has, on more than one occasion, buried her face in her hands during RMB rehearsals.

Anna started dancing at 8 (RAD Ballet, AJDA Jazz, NZAMD Jazz, BBO Tap) and won a scholarship to the **NZ School of Ballet at 12**. She took over her tap teacher's school at 15 (renaming it Marlborough School of Dance), and moved to Queenstown in 2003 to dance and choreograph in *Starry Eyed*, Margaret O'Hanlon and Nigel Hirst's creation that became the genesis of what we now call the Christmas Show.

In **2006**, the year we count as the Remarkable Men's Ballet's founding, she co-founded **Queenstown School of Dance** with Lee van der Schuit. She rebranded it **DEVOS (Dance Evolution Studios)** in 2021 to teach more holistically, focusing on mindful movement and mental wellness as much as technique. At 40, she earned her APDA JazzAddict Solo Seal with Distinction. She's a certified dance examiner for the Asia Pacific Dance Association and a Resident Artist at Te Atamira.

Anna was the **official choreographer of the Queenstown Christmas Show from 2013 to 2025**, and wrestled the RMB into formation from at least 2010. James Beech's 2011 ODT column captured her gentle martyrdom: *"What happened to my quiet little fairies?"* she asked when the men waltzed toward her *"like a herd of crazed elephants."*

For her final year with the men in 2025 she booked the rehearsal space, brought in Claire Hazledine to keep everyone in line, and added a weeknight session when Sundays proved beyond them. Spike called her the slave master; she preferred ring master. The men called her the glue.

Without her, it would have been a brawl. With her, it was just barely ballet.
