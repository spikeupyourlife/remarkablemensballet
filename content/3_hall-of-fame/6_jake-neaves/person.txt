Bio: 

Joined the troupe in 2024, one of two new faces that year. A Queenstown volunteer firefighter by day; tutu-wearer by Christmas.

The 2024 show was the year of the presents (no tutus): Ferg told the ODT the troupe was "still a bit sore" about the tutu absence. Jake made do with corrugated cardboard, a sense of humour and, for good measure, crutches.

Back on two feet for the 2025 black swans, where he also became the troupe's unofficial photographer. Last seen at Queenstown Airport looking about ready to take flight, and already keen for 2026.
