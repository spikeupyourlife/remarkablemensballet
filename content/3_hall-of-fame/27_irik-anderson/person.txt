Bio: **The Viking**, and one of the longest-serving men on the books. Irik was in the Class of 2011 (ODT, *Meet the lords of the dance*), was back in a tutu for the troupe's first ever outside gig, the 2025 Arrowtown Autumn Festival, dancing Claire Hazledine's routine to Vivaldi's *Autumn*, and then danced the black swans at Christmas 2025.

Keeper of troupe lore, sailor when he isn't dancing, and inventor of the emergency paper-cone swan beak. His philosophy, roughly: the hairline may say polish, but the attitude says glitter. Fourteen years between press mentions and not a step forgotten. Allegedly.
