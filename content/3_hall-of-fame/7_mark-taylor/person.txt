Bio: Builder by trade, recruited by Special in late 2025 as **the Waterboy**, and also known to the troupe as **Red**. The name, for the record: he missed the first four practices, so the boys put a water bottle in his spot. The bottle held its position beautifully. Mark has since taken over. Reviews of his first rehearsal were glowing: he shone like the star he is, and was hailed as the new Jasmine. His stated ambition was to deliver mediocrity to the very best of his ability. He made his debut as a black swan at Christmas 2025, after first locating the wrong hall. Will do anything for a buffet.
