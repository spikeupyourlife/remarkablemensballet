Bio: The only person in troupe history to steal from the ballet on stage and be thanked for it. At the 2011 Christmas Show, then-Mayor Vanessa van Uden made off with the men's flowers in full view of the Queenstown Events Centre. Justice was swift and sparkly: she was wrapped in tinsel and carried offstage by several "ballerinas". No charges were laid. The flowers were never recovered.

She came back for more in 2016, belting out "I Will Survive" with the troupe, which by then was less a song choice than a statement of fact. Over the years she has cheered, cameoed and generally outranked everyone in the room, and Pete Laurenson, who met her at boarding school in Dunedin, has been dancing in her shadow ever since.

Farm girl from near Te Anau, former mayor, flower thief, tinsel survivor. By the ancient and entirely made-up powers vested in us, we name her an honorary member. Tutu optional. Flowers, please return.
