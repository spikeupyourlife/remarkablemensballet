Bio: 

Stage name **Petri Dish**, which he chose himself, so nobody else can be blamed. Known to most as **Pete**.

Pete's first brush with the Remarkable Men's Ballet came before he had ever been to a rehearsal: he was roped in for Vanessa van Uden's farewell. The photo has, by his own account, been sent everywhere. He does not know who is holding him up. Someone strong.

He properly joined the troupe as a performer in 2024 alongside Jake Neaves, though the Laurenson household has been in the RMB's orbit for over a decade, thanks to a run of mayoral cameos with the troupe.

Pete was keen as mustard for 2025, but every rehearsal Sunday found him somewhere else entirely, from Ohakune and Melbourne to Gore and Invercargill, so he cheered the swans on from afar. In 2026 he hosted the troupe and declared himself ready to get the tutu on again.
