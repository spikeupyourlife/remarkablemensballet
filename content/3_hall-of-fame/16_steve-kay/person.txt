Bio: Stage name **Betty Swollocks**, and please don't call him Steve while he's in character. Ferg calls him **Yelly**.

Steve is the troupe's hype man, archivist and fixer: he finds the costume references, the school hall and the speakers, and he even dug up video of the troupe's circa-1990 autumn festival forerunners. Part of the six-man line-up that took the troupe beyond the Christmas Show for the first time, at the 2025 Arrowtown Autumn Festival (choreography by Claire Hazledine, music by Vivaldi, nerves by Steve), and a black swan at Christmas 2025, having cut short a work trip to Te Anau rather than miss the flash mob.
