Bio: Full name **Steven Michael Gallagher**, which is far too many names for one tutu, so everyone calls him **Special**.

He signed up for 2025 promising to ruin ballet all over again, then recruited the Waterboy for good measure. Special keeps up his black swan training wherever he happens to be, from Samoa to the top of Cecil Peak, and he keeps the green room stocked with snacks. Christmas 2025 black swan. His golf, we don't ask about.

Special first pulled on a tutu around five years ago, back in the COVID years, and he's certain it was "definitely tutus with Nigel Kerr". His favourite routine in all that time is the bicycle one at the 2022 Christmas Show, chasing the mirror round the stage. (The ODT caught the bicycle on camera: see [Press](/press).) His ballet hero, without hesitation, is the G. The original G. Asked whether he would recommend the troupe to his mates, he gave it some thought and said no.
