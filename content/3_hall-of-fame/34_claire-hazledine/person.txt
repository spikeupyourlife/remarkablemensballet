Bio: The choreographer who took the Remarkable Men's Ballet on tour. Claire created the troupe's 2025 Arrowtown Autumn Festival routine, set to Vivaldi's *Autumn*, for the first time the men had ever performed outside the Christmas Show. Arrowtown invited them back in 2026, which we choose to read as a review of her work rather than ours.

For Christmas 2025 she ran the weeknight rehearsals, made heaps of changes, promised the audience a wow, and taught a dozen grown men how to be swans. She also teaches community dance at Te Atamira. Her parting notes to the men before curtain: sparkle, and shine.
