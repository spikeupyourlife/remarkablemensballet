Bio: 

> *Host of FergCast, veteran of Queenstown radio and council chambers, and the troupe's General, known to all simply as The G.*

Raised in Hamilton, Craig played rugby for the New Zealand Colts and the Junior All Blacks before "keeping Jock Hobbs out of age-group rugby", until he got injured and Hobbs took the chance to become an All Blacks captain. After arriving in Queenstown for a ski holiday in '87, Craig stayed: liftie at Coronet Peak, wine waiter at Skyline, tour driver, then captain of Wakatipu Premiers rugby.

Station boss Chas Drader poached him from the tour bus into a job at Q92FM, which turned into more than 30 years on Queenstown breakfast radio. Add the chair of the Bruce Grant Youth Trust, more community fundraisers MC'd than he can count, and years at the Queenstown Lakes District Council table, and you have a man who has talked his way through most of the town's history.

These days he runs FergCast, his own podcast, while enjoying the gentler moments of age: long chats, good company and no alarm clock. The microphone, it turns out, was never going to let him go.

On the roster since at least 2011, and as of 2024 the troupe's "head mincer". The G picks the line-up, and he is very clear that turning up to rehearsal counts. For 2025 he handed the baton to Spike and watched from the stalls, declaring each show the best one yet. In 2026 he announced the G's comeback tour for the twentieth, with a simple battle cry: bring back the tutu.
