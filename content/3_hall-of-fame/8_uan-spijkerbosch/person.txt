Bio: 

The man who built this website and then, as punishment, was handed the baton. Spike stepped up as acting G for the 2025 Christmas Show: he wrote the rehearsal schedule, wrangled the venues and booked the makeup, all while learning which end of a tutu goes up.

Quoted in the ODT in April 2025 ahead of the Arrowtown Autumn Festival, sounding very ready for it: *"We think we've got it. It's solid, it's dynamic, and we've nailed it."* That routine was Claire Hazledine's, set to Vivaldi's *Autumn*.

Reigning Best Dressed at the troupe's quiz night outing. His own review of the 2025 show photos: a short man doing his very best impression of a tall one.
