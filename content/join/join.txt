Title: Join the Troupe

----

Headline: Auditions are (technically) open

----

Blurb: [DRAFT] The Remarkable Men's Ballet is always looking for men of courage, questionable flexibility and a robust sense of humour. No experience necessary. Actually, experience is slightly discouraged.

----

Requirements:

- Must be a man of Queenstown Lakes (or able to pretend convincingly)
- Must own, or be prepared to borrow, a sense of humour
- Ability to touch toes: optional. Ability to point at toes: required
- Must commit to rehearsals in the weeks before the Christmas Show (dates TBC)
- Facial hair welcome. Leg hair is a matter for your conscience
- Must understand that the tutu chooses you, not the other way round
- Must keep a straight face for at least four consecutive bars

----

Notrequired: Rhythm, coordination, dignity, prior ballet training, a working knowledge of French.

----

Process:

1. Send us an email with your name and your single best dance move (described in words; we are not ready for video)
2. Attend a rigorous audition, which is mostly a cup of tea with Ferg
3. Get measured for a tutu. Try not to cry
4. Rehearse. Rehearse. Forget everything on the night. Be magnificent anyway
