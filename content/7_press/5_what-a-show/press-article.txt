Title: What a show!

----

Excerpt: Two sold-out performances of "A World of Christmas" at the Queenstown Events Centre, featuring the ever-popular Remarkable Men's Ballet and 27 young performers.

----

Body:

> Queenstown's annual Christmas extravaganza took place at the Events Centre on Sunday with two sold-out performances.
>
> The show, titled *"A World of Christmas"*, featured 16 actors and 11 dancers (ages 9–14) exploring Christmas traditions across cultures. The production included performances from Queenstown Gymnastics Club, Silk and Circus, and the Wakatipu Conservatoire of Classical Ballet.
